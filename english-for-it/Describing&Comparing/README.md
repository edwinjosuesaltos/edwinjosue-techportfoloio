# 📝 Countable & Uncountable Nouns

---

## 1️⃣ Determiners for Countable Nouns (Numbers) 📚

> Countable = cosas que puedes contar (books, laptops, apples)

**Ejemplos:**

- I have **three** books on my shelf.  
- She needs **a** new laptop for her work.

---

## 2️⃣ Determiners for Uncountable Nouns (Some / Enough / Little) 💧

> Uncountable = cosas que no se pueden contar (advice, water, respect)

**Ejemplos:**

- He showed me a **little** kindness when I needed it most.  
- I would like **some** advice on this software matter.

---

## 3️⃣ Much & Many 🔢

**Much** → uncountable nouns  

- There is **much** respect in this team.  
- ❌ There are too **much** population in my new city.  
- ✅ There is too **much** population in my new city.

**Many** → countable nouns  

- There are **many** developers working on this project.

---

## 4️⃣ Possessive Determiners 👤

**My, Your, His, Her, Its, Our, Their**  

**Significado:** Indican quién posee algo (funciona para countable & uncountable)

**Ejemplos:**

- My laptop is new.  
- Their advice helped me a lot.

---

## 5️⃣ Few & Little ⏳

- **Countable:** There are **few** opportunities left.  
- **Uncountable:** He has **little** time, so be quick.  
- **None:** She has **none** of the required documents.

---

## 6️⃣ Articles: The / A / An 📝

**The** → cosas específicas  

- *The software helps me manage my tasks efficiently.*

**A / An** → no específico / primera mención  

- *I bought **a** new laptop.*  
- *This is **an** excellent team.*  
- Antes de vocales → *an hour, an honest person*

---

## 7️⃣ Vocabulary & Phrasal Verbs 💡

- **Look** → to see  
- **Look after** → to take care of something  
- **Give** → to transfer something  
- **Give up** → to stop  

---

## 8️⃣ Idioms & Expressions ✨

**Two peas in a pod** → dos cosas muy similares  

- *Game design and software developers are **two peas in a pod** (work well together).*

**Figure out** → entender / resolver  

- *AI tries to **figure out** human behaviour.*

**Come to life** → hacerse real, cobrar vida o energía  

- *Designers and developers work together, so their ideas **come to life.***

---

## ✅ Quick Tips

- **Determiners:** countable → numbers / many / few, uncountable → some / much / little  
- **Possessive determiners:** my / your / his / her / its / our / their → ambos tipos de sustantivos  
- **Articles:** a / an → no específico, the → específico  
- **Idioms & phrasal verbs:** aprende en contexto para sonar natural
